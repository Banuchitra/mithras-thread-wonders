Run the AIDLC **Inception** phase for this project. Read `CLAUDE.md` and anything already in `aidlc/` first.

1. Interview me in small rounds (max 4 questions at a time). Don't assume — ask. Cover:
   - Brand: name, story, tone of voice, logo (exists or needed), social/contact channels.
   - Audience: who buys, where they are, how they find the shop.
   - Visual design: colour palette, typography, mood/references, photo style. Offer 2–3 options
     with a short rationale each and check text contrast (WCAG AA). The choice is mine.
   - Pages and v1 scope: what's in, what's explicitly out.
   - Product data: fields per product, categories, how many products at launch.
   - How customers order/contact (no payments in v1).
2. Write `aidlc/00-intent.md` (problem, users, success criteria, constraints).
3. Write `aidlc/01-requirements.md` (user stories with acceptance criteria, non-functional reqs:
   $0, a11y, performance, SEO) and a `## Brand & design` section recording my decisions.
4. Propose the unit breakdown (U1…) with one-line scopes.
5. Stop and ask me to approve. Don't start Construction.
